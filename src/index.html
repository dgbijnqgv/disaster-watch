      Disaster Watch version {{version}}

      Shows natural disasters around the world, tells you which ones could affect you, and what to do about them.

      Made by Lucas Horton, Xiaofeng Ding and Ismael Amanullah.

      Made with JavaScript, HTML and CSS. The map uses Leaflet; the desktop app uses Electron and the Android app uses Capacitor.

      Disaster Watch is an awareness tool, not an official warning service. In an emergency follow instructions from local authorities and call 911 / 112.
